Capabilities

Everything your AI
should actually do.

01

Persistent Memory

Prodigy remembers your conversations across sessions — recent context and saved summaries, with relevant memories retrieved when you need them. Your saved transcript keeps the detailed record.

02

Document-Aware Q&A

Upload PDFs, notes, and research papers once. Ask questions about them across sessions. Prodigy retrieves the right context using semantic search across your entire knowledge base.

03

Calendar Integration

Connected to Google Calendar. Ask "What's on my schedule tomorrow?" or "When is my next deadline?" and get a real answer, not a generic response.

04

Web Search Routing

Prodigy identifies requests that need current information and separates them from document questions. Live web search is not connected yet; the assistant tells you when it cannot verify current information.

05

Task Management

See pending and completed Google Tasks, filter your lists, and ask Prodigy to help you prioritize. Google access is read only; create tasks, edit deadlines and mark them done in Google Tasks.

06

Fully Personalized

Set a custom system prompt, configure your profile, and tune how Prodigy responds. It's your assistant — it should behave the way you want.

How it works

A familiar workflow.
With more context.

01

Sign in with Google

Authenticate securely with your Google account. Your profile, calendar access, and data are linked to you — and only you.

02

Upload your knowledge

Drop in PDFs, notes, or project documentation. Prodigy parses, chunks, and embeds them into a private vector database for instant retrieval.

03

Ask anything

Prodigy routes each query to the right source — your documents, your calendar, its memory of past conversations, or general knowledge — and synthesizes a grounded answer.

04

It gets better over time

Conversations are summarized and stored. Frequently accessed information is prioritized. Prodigy learns the shape of how you work.

Three layers. One continuous conversation.

Conversation

Recent messages keep the current exchange coherent. The transcript stays saved to your account.

Short term

Summaries capture useful preferences, projects and decisions. Relevant notes are brought into later replies.

Long term

Less-used notes move out of the immediate context. They can still be found, and return when relevant again.
